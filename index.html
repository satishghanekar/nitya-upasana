          How to print

          Language
          Dhrupad (धृ)
          Page
          Print font

          Every text starts on a new page. Paper: Letter For Landscape, pick it in Safari's dialog too. Pick A4 paper in Safari's dialog too. Chrome prints it best.

          Printing does not open from the Home Screen app on iPhone. Tap Open in Safari: your choices go with it and the printout is made ready there. If Safari does not open, copy the link and paste it into Safari.
          This browser did not open the print dialog. Tap the browser's Share button and choose Print. If this page opened inside another app, tap Open in Safari: your choices go with it.

          Add to Home Screen
          1. Tap the Share button (at the bottom in Safari, top right in Chrome).
          2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
          3. Tap Add.
          1. Open this page in Safari or Chrome (⋯ or Share → Open in Safari/Chrome).
          2. Then Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
          1. Open Chrome's ⋮ menu (top right).
          2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
          3. Tap Install or Add.
          1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or choose Install from the browser menu.
          1. Click the Share button in Safari's toolbar, or open the File menu.
          2. Choose Add to Dock, then click Add.
          1. Firefox can't add this site as an app. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Safari and tap this button again.

          It will then open like an app, even without internet.
