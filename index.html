      • On the day itself, the card shows the pooja time and how long until moonrise.

      Buttons at the top

      • Home (Ganesha and title): back to the start. While you read, the title shows the piece on screen (for example ५. दत्ताची आरती).
      • Contents and search (lines with a magnifier): jump to any aarti, or type a few letters of a line in Marathi or English to find it. Plain letters are enough, and spelling is forgiving: sukhakarta, ganpati and vithal find sukhakartā, Ganapati and Vitthal. The matching words are shown in bold; tap a result to go to that line. On a computer you can type straight away, and use ↑ ↓ and Enter.
      • अ/A (language): switches the aarti text between Marathi, English, and both together.
      • धृ (dhrupad): show the refrain once at the top, or repeated after every stanza. In “once” mode, tap ↻ ध्रुपद under a stanza to see the refrain for a few seconds.
      • ⚙ Settings: menu language, English style, text size, theme, high contrast, keeping the screen on, and one or two columns on wide screens. See below.

      Settings

      • Menu language: English or मराठी for the date card, buttons and settings. The aartis follow the अ/A button instead.
      • English style: how the English lines and the titles of the pieces are spelled.
        • Sukhkarta: the everyday spelling most of us read easily.
        • sukhakartā: the scholarly spelling (IAST), with exact sounds: ā ī ū for long vowels, ṭ ḍ ṇ for the hard sounds, ś ṣ for the two kinds of “sh”. In the Atharvashirsha it also shows the swara marks: a line under a syllable is the low note (anudātta) and a tick above is the svarita (bha̱draṃ karṇe̍bhiḥ).
        It changes straight away and keeps your place.
      • Text size (A− / A+ / ↺ to reset) and Theme (Auto, Light, Sepia, Dark).
      • High contrast and Keep screen on: switches; tap to turn on or off. Keep screen on starts on for phones and tablets and off on computers.
      • At the top, beside the title: the version number (v1.0), App (until it is installed), Help, and ✕ to close Settings.
      • Layout (wide screens only): one or two columns of stanzas.

      While reading

      • Tap a line to highlight it, so everyone can keep their place. Tap again to clear.
      • Pinch or use A− / A+ in Settings to change the text size of the aartis (the home page stays readable at its normal size). The bubble shows the size (100% is the normal size for your screen); right after a pinch, tap ↺ Default size to go back to normal.
      • Back: your phone's Back button (or swipe) closes an open menu or box, then goes back to where you were before the last jump, for example from an aarti back to the contents.
      • Bottom bar (on every page): ‹ Prev and Next › go to the neighbouring pieces (on the home page, Next opens the first piece); the Ganesha (home) and contents-and-search buttons work just like the ones at the top. Like the top bar it keeps its size when you change the text size or pinch, and it steps aside while Settings or Contents is open.
      • The screen stays on while the site is open (you can turn this off in Settings).

      Share, QR and Print

      These sit in one row at the bottom of the top card on the home page, with App and Help. On narrow phones the row shows icons only.

      • Share sends a ready reminder with the next date, pooja time, moonrise and the link, for WhatsApp or messages.
      • QR shows a code others can scan with their camera to open the site.
      • Print makes a printout or PDF of the contents and all the texts, in the language and English style you have chosen, in one or two columns, or Marathi and English side by side.

      Good to know

      • Saved for offline (the line under the Share/QR/Print buttons): the site is saved on your phone and works without internet. The number after it (for example v1.0) is the version on your phone. Tap Check for updates to look for a newer version: it shows “Checking…”, then “Up to date” in green, or reloads with the new version. “No internet” in amber means the saved copy is in use.
      • Getting updates: tap Check for updates on the home card, or close the site or app and open it again; new changes load in the background.
      • Privacy: your settings and location stay on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
      • Moonrise times are calculated on your phone and may differ by a few minutes from printed calendars.
